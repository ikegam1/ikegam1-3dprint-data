# PETG mesh slippers / 27cm / Flashforge Adventurer 5M

## Important safety
These are **prototype indoor slippers**, not certified protective footwear. PETG is stiff and may be slippery on indoor flooring; its mesh edges can abrade skin and crack under repeated bending. **Attach non-slip rubber pads to the sole before walking, and use socks.** Do not use on wet or slippery floors or stairs. If the seams move, the strap pinches, or any part cracks, stop using them. TPU is strongly preferred if you later acquire it.

## Size / geometry
- Designed as nominal 27 cm (270mm long) pair, approximate widest 106mm. Foot size 27cm does not guarantee fit: actual foot width and instep vary.
- 8mm-thick sole with ventilation holes, front and heel interlocking as a 14mm-long half lap around x=133–147mm.
- Two 2.1mm-radius vertical locating pegs on front half enter 2.55mm-radius holes on the heel half (radial 0.45mm fit clearance).
- One rigid mesh-free wide instep arch strap design, printed twice. The arch is firm PETG, **not an elastic band**.
- Four individual sole STLs plus one strap STL (print strap twice). All STL meshes are watertight and fit 220 x 220mm bed.

## Suggested PETG slicer settings
Orca-Flashforge / Adventurer 5M / 0.4mm nozzle; PETG profile from your filament manufacturer; initial typical nozzle 235–245°C and bed 75–85°C; 0.2mm layers; 4 walls; 25% infill; moderate speed. Sole print as oriented (flat side on bed). Arch prints on its side as oriented, 32mm tall. Preview for unwanted supports and overhangs at arches before production. Do not resize.

## First print / required external supplies
- Start with only one `RIGHT_FRONT` and `RIGHT_HEEL` pair, and confirm the pegs align and the lap closes without force.
- Assemble both halves; **add two durable cable ties across the seam**, through the paired slots near x127–153 at each outer edge. The locating pegs alone do not safely hold parts together when walking. Trim tie ends flush so they cannot scratch feet.
- Place each arch strap across the instep area, test fit over socks. Strap holes are intended for M3 machine fasteners with washers; **do not rely on press-fitting or adhesive only.** Secure with washers and locknuts, keep all protruding hardware away from skin and floor. For a full strap use 4 sets of M3 fasteners per foot. Align by corresponding drilled holes in the front sole.
- Apply rubber/non-slip pads underneath, at heel and forefoot, with appropriate adhesive. Do not cover the mesh openings; pads and adhesive are not supplied.
- Sand/deburr all surfaces that contact feet. Try stationary fit before any walking.

## Limits and next steps
Real-world fit, comfort, non-slip performance, fastener security and fatigue life **have not been tested**. Printed materials may have local size deviations. PLA is not recommended for flexible joints either. The supplied 27cm model is a printable concept to iterate after a low-cost fit test.
